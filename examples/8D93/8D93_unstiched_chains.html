8D93_unstiched: one ASU at chain resolution (R3:H)

Nodes are the chains/TER segments of the original asymmetric unit. Every edge is one symmetry orbit of covalent bonds or stitched joins: X → Y labelled g means X of the original ASU is joined to the copy of Y made by symmetry code g written op_(k,l,m): the operation number, then a signed whole-cell shift along a, b and c, so 2_(0,+1,0) is operation 2 followed by +1 cell along b and (0,0,0) is no shift; unlabelled = inside the ASU. The details panel also gives the PDB form, op_klm with each shift + 5. Self-loops join a node to another copy of itself. Composing the codes around any cycle gives the operation the real lattice path ends on; a non-identity result means the graph continues through the crystal. Dashed purple edges are same-polarity (5′-5′ or 3′-3′) chain-end stacks. Hover or tap an edge or node for details; drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Periodicity (all edges):
