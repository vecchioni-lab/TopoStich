8D93_unstiched: one ASU at residue resolution (R3:H)

Nodes are the residues of the original asymmetric unit. Every edge is one symmetry orbit of covalent bonds or stitched joins: X → Y labelled g means X of the original ASU is joined to the copy of Y made by symmetry code g (op_klm; 555 = no cell shift; unlabelled = inside the ASU). Self-loops join a node to another copy of itself. Composing the codes around any cycle gives the operation the real lattice path ends on; a non-identity result means the graph continues through the crystal.
